P03 – Mismatches in Binding and Coreference

Go to next subproject

Project description

Project P03 investigates two empirical domains: fake indexicals and other bound pronouns, and unbound pronouns exhibiting covariance. It examines their morphological, syntactic, and semantic properties within a model where syntax mediates between LF and PF by transferring features exhaustively or redundantly. Fake indexicals are analyzed as involving an R operation from syntax to LF, whereas unbound pronouns involve an R operation from syntax to PF. The phenomena thus form mirror images within the CRC’s R/D framework.

The project examines pronoun structure, feature distribution across grammar, mismatches between components, and possible feature sources. It tests two hypotheses: fake indexicals are syntactically bound through the genuine indexical via focus binding, while unbound pronouns are definite noun phrases that undergo ellipsis.

P03 aims to formalize redundancy/deficiency mechanisms and includes a detailed study of fake indexicals in Romance and Slavic languages. Its broad empirical and theoretical scope supports collaboration with most other CRC subprojects.

Franziska Keller

Franziska Keller holds a BSc in General Theoretical Linguistics from the University of Potsdam and an MA in General Linguistics from the University of Vienna. Her research focuses on binding phenomena and fake indexicals, alongside the morphology–syntax interface, root-and-pattern morphology, Arabic languages, resumptive pronouns, and syntactic movement.
