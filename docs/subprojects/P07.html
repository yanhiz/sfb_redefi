P07 — Deficiency and Redundancy in Iberoromance Pronominal Systems

Project description

This subproject investigates clitic doubling and null objects across Spanish and Portuguese varieties, with a focus on River Plate Spanish and Mineiro Brazilian Portuguese. It examines whether the observed microvariation and gradient acceptability can be explained through the SFB concepts of redundancy and deficiency, including the influence of discourse factors such as familiarity and D-linking.

The project also develops a unified account of these constructions across Iberoromance and evaluates existing proposals, including Maddox’s (2021) Object Agreement Cycle and the Clitic Doubling Parameter proposed by Fischer et al. (2019).

Institutional profile

Philippa Adolf

PhD student
University of Vienna

Philippa Adolf is a researcher at the Department of Romance Linguistics at University of Vienna. Her research focusses on Spanish syntax, specifically differential object marking, clitic doubling, and the null-instantiation of objects. She also has a particular interest in empirical methods and statistic modelling of linguistic data due to her background in corpus-based cognitive linguistics. Read more
